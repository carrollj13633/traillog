TrailLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

TrailLog is a GPS trail recorder for Android and the web. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. The short version: your recordings stay on your phone. The developer does not receive them.

What TrailLog is

TrailLog records your path from your phone's GPS while you hike, run, ride, or drive. It draws the path on a map and shows distance, time, speed, and elevation. You can save, rename, delete, import, and export tracks.

Information TrailLog uses

Location. When you tap Start, TrailLog collects your phone's precise location (latitude, longitude, altitude, speed, accuracy, and time). It keeps collecting while a recording is running, even when the app is closed or the screen is off, so your trail has no gaps. While this is happening, Android shows a notification, "TrailLog is recording". Collection stops when you tap Pause or Stop. TrailLog does not collect location when you are not recording, except to show your position on the map while the app is open.

Your saved tracks and settings. Recorded points, track names, and preferences (units, theme, map layer) are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it. TrailLog turns off Android's cloud backup for this data, so it is not copied to your Google account.

Information TrailLog does not collect

  • The developer has no server for TrailLog. Your location and tracks are never uploaded to the developer or to anyone else by the app.
  • There are no accounts, sign-ins, advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting tools in TrailLog.
  • TrailLog does not sell or share your data.

Map pictures (tiles) from third parties

The map is made of small pictures (tiles) that TrailLog downloads over the internet from third-party map providers: OpenStreetMap, Esri (satellite), and OpenTopoMap (topographic). To send you the pictures, these providers receive your device's IP address and the map area being viewed, which is usually near your location. They handle that information under their own privacy policies:

  • OpenStreetMap Foundation: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
  • Esri: https://www.esri.com/en-us/privacy/overview
  • OpenTopoMap: https://opentopomap.org

Everything else in TrailLog works without an internet connection. If you do not want map requests, use the app offline, and your recordings still work (the map area will be blank).

Sharing and exporting

TrailLog shares a file only when you ask it to. Export and Share create a GPX or KML file and open the Android share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example, email, Drive, or a messaging app). That app's own privacy policy then applies to the file. Exported files may show where you were and when, so check before you share a track from your home or other private places.

Permissions TrailLog asks for

  • Location (precise and approximate): to record your trail.
  • Location "all the time" (background): so recording continues with the screen off or another app open. This is optional. TrailLog still collects location only while a recording is running.
  • Notifications: to show the "TrailLog is recording" notification while a recording runs.
  • Internet: to download map tiles.

You can change or revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings > Apps > TrailLog > Permissions.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your tracks stay on your phone until you delete them. To delete one, open it in TrailLog and tap Delete. To delete everything, clear TrailLog's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Because the developer holds no copy, nobody else can restore deleted tracks. Export a GPX file if you want a backup.

Children

TrailLog is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and it sends none to the developer.

Security

Recordings are kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open TrailLog and see your tracks, so use a screen lock.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same web address with a new "Last updated" date. If a change affects how location is used, the app will ask you again.
