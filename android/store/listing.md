## Full description (max 4,000 characters)
TrailLog records your hikes, runs, rides, and drives from your phone's GPS, then keeps every track private on your phone. No account. No ads. No tracking.

Tap Start and go. TrailLog keeps recording with the screen off, so you can put your phone in your pocket and enjoy the trail.

RECORD
• Big Start, Pause, Resume, and Stop buttons
• Live map with your position and a line that grows as you move
• Live stats: time, distance, current and average speed, elevation gain
• Switch between miles and kilometers
• Cleans up GPS noise so jitter and bad fixes do not add fake distance
• Crash-safe: every point is saved as it arrives, and TrailLog offers to resume if the app is interrupted

REVIEW
• Saved tracks with name, date, distance, and duration
• Map view with start and end markers and a green-to-red speed path
• Elevation profile you can scrub along
• Rename and delete tracks

MAPS
• Street, Satellite, and Topo map layers
• Everything except the map pictures works offline

EXPORT AND SHARE
• Export GPX 1.1 and KML 2.2 files, for example for Google Earth, Google My Maps, and other trail apps
• Import GPX tracks
• Send a track with the Android share sheet

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your recordings are stored only on your phone. TrailLog has no server and no account, and never uploads your location.
• No ads, no analytics, no trackers

LOCATION IN THE BACKGROUND
TrailLog collects precise location while a recording is running, including when the app is closed or the screen is off, so your trail has no gaps. Android shows a "TrailLog is recording" notification the whole time, and collection stops when you tap Pause or Stop. TrailLog never collects location in the background when you are not recording.

TIPS FOR LONG RECORDINGS
• Choose "Allow all the time" for location when asked
• Set TrailLog's battery use to Unrestricted (TrailLog guides you)
• Do not swipe TrailLog away from your recent apps while recording

Map pictures are downloaded from OpenStreetMap, Esri, and OpenTopoMap when you view the map.
